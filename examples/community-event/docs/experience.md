# Experience: fictional community welcome guide

## People and goals
A guest wants to arrive at the right place and find help without needing a phone. A volunteer wants guests with uncertain information to reach the welcome desk.

## Main journey
The guest reads Arrival, goes to Riverside Community Hall's main entrance, and checks in at the welcome desk. The desk gives current room directions.

## Other outcomes
If confirmation details are missing, the guide's Missing details section directs the guest to the welcome desk. If an old copy lists an outdated room, the Room changes section tells them to ask the desk for current directions.

## Accessibility and practical use
Use descriptive text headings and short paragraphs. Essential directions must be in the text, without relying on a hyperlink, QR code, or color. This sample does not claim validated large-print layout or user-tested accessibility.

## Wording and thresholds
Use the labels Arrival, Missing details, and Room changes. Refer consistently to the welcome desk at the main entrance.

## Assumptions and open questions
The fictional venue has a welcome desk. No other questions are needed for the limited content check.
