# Approach: project name

## Overview
Explain how the result will be produced and maintained.

## Parts and responsibilities
Name each part, person, document, or system; what it owns; and what it depends on.

## Information flow
Describe what is handed from one part to another and where the authoritative information lives.

## Decisions and tradeoffs
Explain consequential choices, their reasons, and relevant alternatives.

## Failure and recovery
What can go wrong, who notices, and how can work recover?

## Constraints, assumptions, and open questions
Preserve the scope's constraints and distinguish unresolved choices from decisions.
